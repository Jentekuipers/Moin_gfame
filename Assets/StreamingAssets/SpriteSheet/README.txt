MOIN sprite sheet (802 sprites)

Edit a copy of sheet_original.png and save it as sheet.png (keep the size and layout),
and/or put single sprites in overrides/<name>.png (any size; names as in sprites/).
The game loads them on the next start. Delete them to go back to the built-in art.
Tiles (dirt, stone...) are painted per pixel and aren't on the sheet.
